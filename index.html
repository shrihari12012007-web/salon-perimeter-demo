Clinical Optometry & Care

Personalized Eye Care for Gangavathi

Focused, unhurried consultations in an intimate clinic environment. We take the time to test your vision thoroughly and ensure your glasses fit comfortably.

01 Daily Clinic

Computerized Vision Examination

High-accuracy automated objective refraction using digital auto-refractometers, followed by detailed subjective trial frame testing for spherical, cylinder, and axis verification.

  • Refraction for Distance & Near Power
  • Astigmatism / Cylinder Angle Tuning
  • Eye Muscle Balance & Binocular Focus
02 Precision Lab

Designer Frame Selection & Fitting

Professional facial shape analysis to help you pick the ideal spectacle shape—whether you need lightweight titanium for all-day office wear or bold acetate for personal style.

  • Panto angle & vertex distance calibration
  • Rimless, Half-Rim & Full-Rim fitting
  • Free lifetime nose pad & screw maintenance
03 Specialist

Progressive & Bifocal Clinic

Struggling with reading small print on your phone or books after age 40? We customize corridor-free progressive lenses allowing natural vision from driving to reading without swapping glasses.

  • Zero distortion wide viewing corridors
  • Crizal & Essilor progressive options
  • Adaptation guarantee & fitting counseling
04 Digital Eye

Blue-Cut Screen Protection

Combat eye fatigue, dry eyes, burning sensations, and late-night insomnia caused by mobile and computer screen exposure with premium blue-light absorbing lenses.

  • Zero yellowish tint distortion
  • UV400 electromagnetic protection
  • Anti-reflective night driving filter
05 Contact Lens

Contact Lens Clinic & Fitting

Soft daily, monthly, and toric contact lenses for sports, weddings, and everyday comfort. Includes initial trial, insertion training, and hygienic lens care solutions.

  • High-oxygen breathable hydrogel lenses
  • Toric lenses for astigmatic eyes
  • Cosmetic colored lenses with power
06 Preliminary

Eye Health & Pressure Screening

Preliminary anterior segment inspection under slit lamp magnification, non-contact intraocular pressure check, and referral guidance for advanced cataract or retina conditions.

  • Corneal clarity & conjunctiva check
  • Early cataract clouding assessment
  • Foreign body & eye irritation evaluation

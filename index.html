ABOUT SALON PERIMETER

A Thoughtfully Curated Haven for Women & Their Little Ones

Located on Sharavathi Nagar Main Road in Shivamogga, Salon Perimeter is founded on a singular philosophy: every woman deserves a peaceful, personalized beauty experience, and every child deserves gentle, stress-free grooming.

We understand that a salon visit is more than just a trim—it is your time to unwind, rejuvenate, and feel cared for. Our team brings dedicated attention to every strand, recommending customized hair therapies, sophisticated styling, and restorative treatments tailored to your unique hair type and lifestyle.

Comfort & Privacy

A welcoming, feminine-friendly environment where you can relax in complete comfort.

Gentle Pediatric Hair Care

Patient, friendly hairstylists who take the time to make kids' haircuts fun, easy, and tear-free.

Attentive Hair Consultations

We listen closely to your hair goals, scalp conditions, and preferences before beginning any treatment.

Strict Hygiene Protocols

Sanitized tools, fresh disposable salon capes, and clean styling stations for every client.

ABOUT SALON PERIMETER

A Thoughtfully Curated Haven for Women & Their Little Ones

Located on Sharavathi Nagar Main Road in Shivamogga, Salon Perimeter is created with a dedicated focus: providing a tranquil, private beauty space for women, along with gentle, tear-free hair care for children.

A salon visit should be unhurried and rejuvenating. Whether you are looking for haircut maintenance, restorative hair spa care, or bringing your child for their school haircut, our environment is designed for privacy, warmth, and attentive personal consultation.

Comfort & Privacy

A welcoming, feminine-friendly setting where women and mothers feel completely at ease.

Gentle Pediatric Hair Care

Patient, friendly hairstylists who take the extra time to keep children calm, happy, and comfortable.

Attentive Consultations

We listen closely to your hair requirements, routine, and preferences before beginning any treatment.

Clean & Sanitized Tools

Clean workstations, sanitized equipment, and fresh salon capes for every guest.
